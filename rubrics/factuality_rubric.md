# Factuality Rubric

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Accurate, evidence-aware, and appropriately cautious. |
| 4 | Mostly accurate with minor missing context. |
| 3 | Some unsupported claims or weak uncertainty handling. |
| 2 | Multiple unsupported claims or misleading specificity. |
| 1 | Fabricated facts, fake citations, or high-impact misinformation. |

## Checks

- Does the answer invent sources, names, dates, laws, or metrics?
- Does it distinguish known facts from assumptions?
- Does it ask for missing source material when required?
- Are sensitive domains handled with caution?
