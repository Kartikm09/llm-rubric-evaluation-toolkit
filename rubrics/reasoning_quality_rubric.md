# Reasoning Quality Rubric

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Logic is complete, consistent, and easy to follow. |
| 4 | Minor reasoning gap but conclusion is reliable. |
| 3 | Partially reasoned; missing steps or assumptions. |
| 2 | Contradictory or shallow reasoning. |
| 1 | Invalid reasoning or conclusion does not follow. |

## Reviewer Questions

- Are the steps in the right order?
- Are assumptions visible?
- Does the conclusion match the evidence?
- Does the model skip necessary calculations or comparisons?
