# Tone and Clarity Rubric

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Clear, concise, professional, and matched to user context. |
| 4 | Clear but slightly verbose or generic. |
| 3 | Understandable but awkward, long, or unfocused. |
| 2 | Hard to follow or tone mismatch. |
| 1 | Confusing, dismissive, or inappropriate tone. |

## Checks

- Does the response lead with the answer?
- Is the wording natural and readable?
- Is the tone respectful?
- Is the response too long for the task?
